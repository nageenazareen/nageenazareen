## About

I'm Nageena Zareen, founder of [Evox AI Tech](https://evoxaitech.com), based in Islamabad, Pakistan.

At Evox, we build AI systems that take repetitive work off a business's plate: receptionists that answer customers around the clock, voice agents that handle calls, and automations that connect the tools a team already uses. We work with clinics, restaurants, hotels and service businesses in Pakistan and the Gulf.

I lead product and engineering at Evox. Alongside the company, I'm completing my degree in Computer Science, and mobile app development with Flutter is my core field.
